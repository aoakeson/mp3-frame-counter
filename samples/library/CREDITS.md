# Example MP3 credits

Every recording and cover image here is in the public domain or dedicated to it with CC0, so they can be
copied, played and modified without permission or attribution. Credit is given anyway. Files are byte-for-byte
as downloaded from Wikimedia Commons (renamed only), so their frame counts can be checked against the originals.
Covers are center-cropped to 256×256.

U.S. military band and NASA recordings are works of the U.S. federal government. Musopen recordings are CC0.
James Brigham released his Joplin performances into the public domain.
